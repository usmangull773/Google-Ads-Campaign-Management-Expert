01

What we found

Your campaigns are several years old and you have asked for two things first: a retargeting campaign aimed at people with real buying intent, and an answer on the invalid clicks you are seeing. Both come down to the same root cause, which is who the account is allowed to show ads to and how it counts them.

Retargeting needs to be built on intent, not on visits

High impact

Most older accounts retarget everyone who touched the site. For a glass and mirror shop, someone who read the shower door page and opened the quote form is a very different buyer from someone who landed on the homepage for three seconds. Splitting audiences by the page they viewed and the action they took is what makes retargeting pay.

Typical old setup
  • One list of all site visitors
  • Same ad shown to every visitor
  • Short visits and job seekers included
Intent based setup
  • Lists by service page viewed
  • Ad message matched to that service
  • Quote starters and callers prioritized

Invalid clicks need evidence, then exclusions

High impact

If someone is clicking your ads on purpose, the account will show patterns: repeat clicks from the same areas, odd hours, specific placements or devices, and visits with no time on site. We document those patterns first, so you have a clear record for a Google invalid click review, then lock the account down.

Each search should land on the page that answers it

Medium impact

Someone searching for frameless shower doors should land on the shower door page, not the homepage. Matching ad groups to service pages lifts conversion rate and makes the retargeting lists cleaner, because every visit is already sorted by service.
